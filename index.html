Welcome

Welcome to the Student Profile & Course Portal! This platform is created to help students find their academic information in a simple and organized way. Explore the portal and discover useful resources for your student journey.

Introduction

This Portal is a simple HTML-only project built using only semantic HTML5 tags.It demonstrates how to structure a multi-page website using proper elements like header, nav, main, section, article, aside and footer.Feel free to explore each page from the nav. Use the navigations links to visit the PROFILE, view the list of COURSES, submit FEEDBACK , check your grades GRADES , or get in touch throughh the CONTACT page.

Quick Tip

Keep your academic information updated and regularly check your courses and grades. Use the Feedback page whenever you want to share your suggestions or experience.
